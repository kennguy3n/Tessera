Prepared by: David Reyes, Paralegal — Corporate Group, Hartwell & Cho LLP
Supervising attorney: Jordan Pak, Partner
Matter: NW-2026-0033 (Northwind Outdoor Co. — vendor contracts)

## Open issues flagged by supervising attorney

1. **Liability cap (§5.1):** Partner wants the cap raised to **2× annual fees**
   for data-related claims. Current draft caps at 1× trailing-12-month fees.
   Acme has historically agreed to 1.5× as a compromise.
2. **Breach notification (§4.3):** 72 hours is acceptable, but we want it tied to
   "discovery" not "confirmation," and we want Acme to bear the cost of customer
   notifications if the breach is their fault. Not yet in the draft.
3. **Auto-renewal (§1.2):** 60-day non-renewal window is tight for a 24-month
   deal. Calendar the notice deadline so Northwind doesn't auto-renew by accident.
4. **Fee escalator (§2.3):** 5% is on the high side; market is 3-4%. Low priority.

## Risk posture

- Northwind is the customer and the paying party; payment obligations are
  uncapped (§5.2), which is acceptable.
- The SLA service-credit "sole remedy" (§3.3) means no termination right for
  chronic downtime — partner wants a **termination-for-repeated-SLA-failure**
  right (e.g., 3 months below 99.0% in any rolling 12 months). Not yet drafted.
