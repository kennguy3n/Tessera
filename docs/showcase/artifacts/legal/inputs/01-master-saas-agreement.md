# Master Software-as-a-Service Agreement (excerpts)

**Agreement title:** Master SaaS Agreement
**Customer:** Northwind Outdoor Co., a Delaware corporation ("Customer")
**Vendor:** Acme Cloud, Inc., a California corporation ("Provider")
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2026

## 1. Term and Renewal
1.1 The initial term is **twenty-four (24) months** from the Effective Date.
1.2 The Agreement **auto-renews for successive 12-month periods** unless either
party gives written notice of non-renewal at least **sixty (60) days** before
the end of the then-current term.

## 2. Fees and Payment
2.1 Annual subscription fee: **$480,000**, invoiced annually in advance.
2.2 Payment due **net 30** from invoice date. Late amounts accrue interest at
1.5%/month.
2.3 Fees increase by no more than **5%** per renewal term.

## 3. Service Levels
3.1 Provider will maintain **99.9% monthly uptime** for the production service.
3.2 If uptime falls below 99.9% in a month, Customer receives service credits:
10% of monthly fee for <99.9%, 25% for <99.0%, 50% for <95.0%.
3.3 Service credits are Customer's **sole remedy** for availability failures.

## 4. Data Protection
4.1 Provider processes Customer Data solely to provide the service.
4.2 Provider maintains SOC 2 Type II certification and encrypts data in transit
and at rest.
4.3 Provider will notify Customer of a confirmed data breach **within 72 hours**.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
5.1 Except for the Excluded Claims, each party's aggregate liability is capped at
**the fees paid in the 12 months** preceding the claim.
5.2 Excluded Claims (uncapped): breach of confidentiality, indemnification
obligations, and Customer's payment obligations.
5.3 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.

## 6. Indemnification
6.1 Provider indemnifies Customer against third-party claims that the service
infringes IP rights.
6.2 Customer indemnifies Provider against claims arising from Customer Data.

## 7. Termination
7.1 Either party may terminate for material breach uncured after **30 days**
written notice.
7.2 On termination, Provider returns or deletes Customer Data within 30 days and
provides an export in a machine-readable format.

## 8. Governing Law
8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **State of New York**, without
regard to conflicts principles. Venue: state and federal courts in New York County.
