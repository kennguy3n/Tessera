# Funder RFP Summary & Board Context

## Funder: The Halverson Family Foundation
- **Priority:** Closing the STEM opportunity gap for low-income students in grades 4-8.
- **Grant range:** $75,000-$250,000 over 12-24 months.
- **What they fund:** program expansion with a credible evaluation plan; they
  favor matched funding and sustainability beyond the grant period.
- **What they will NOT fund:** capital construction, endowment, or deficit reduction.
- **Required:** measurable outcomes, a logic model, and a sustainability plan.
- **Deadline:** proposals due 2026-04-15; decisions by 2026-06-30.

## Selection criteria (weighting)
- Demonstrated outcomes / evidence base — 35%
- Organizational capacity — 25%
- Evaluation plan — 20%
- Sustainability & matching funds — 20%

## Board context (for the board update deck)
- RYC FY2025 operating budget: $2.4M; BrightPath is ~18% of program spend.
- The board's strategic plan names "deepen STEM access" as a top-3 priority.
- Development pipeline: $640K identified for FY2026; $185K Halverson ask is the
  single largest open opportunity.
- Last year RYC closed 71% of its grant pipeline by dollar value.
- Board asked for: clearer outcome metrics, a diversified funder mix (reduce
  reliance on the top 2 funders, currently 44% of revenue), and a multi-year view.
