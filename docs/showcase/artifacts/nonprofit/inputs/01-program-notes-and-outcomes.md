# Program Notes & Outcomes Data — After-School STEM Program

Org: Riverside Youth Coalition (RYC)
Prepared by: Sofia Alvarez, Development Director
Program: "BrightPath" after-school STEM enrichment

## Program description

BrightPath serves students in grades 4-8 at three Title I schools in Riverside.
Sessions run 3:30-5:30pm, four days a week, October through May. Activities:
hands-on robotics, coding, and applied-math labs, plus homework support and a
healthy snack. Staffed by 2 full-time coordinators and 11 part-time instructors,
many of them local college STEM majors.

## Reach (2024-25 school year)

- Students enrolled: **214** (capacity 240).
- Average daily attendance: **78%**.
- Demographics: 62% qualify for free/reduced lunch; 48% female; 5 home languages.
- Waitlist at year end: **96 students** (demand exceeds capacity).

## Outcomes (2024-25)

- Math proficiency: participants improved an average of **11 percentile points**
  on the district benchmark vs. +3 for matched non-participants.
- School-day attendance among participants rose from 91% to **95%**.
- 89% of caregivers surveyed reported their child was "more interested in school."
- 7 students advanced to the regional FIRST LEGO League tournament; 1 team placed 2nd.

## What we want to fund (the ask)

- Expand from 3 to **5 schools**, adding ~150 student slots.
- Hire 1 additional full-time coordinator and 6 part-time instructors.
- Purchase 5 robotics kits/classroom and refresh laptops.
- **Total request: $185,000 over 18 months.** RYC will match 20% from reserves
  and a corporate sponsor (Northwind Outdoor Co. community fund).
