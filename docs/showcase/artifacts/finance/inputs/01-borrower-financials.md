## Borrower profile
- **Borrower:** Riverbend Logistics LLC, a regional less-than-truckload (LTL) carrier.

- **Ownership:** 70% Elena Marsh (CEO), 30% Tomas Reyes (COO). Both personally guaranteeing.

## Facility requested
- **Type:** Equipment term loan
- **Amount:** **$3,200,000**
- **Purpose:** Purchase 18 new tractors and 24 trailers to replace aging fleet and add two regional lanes.
- **Term:** 60 months, monthly amortization.
- **Proposed rate:** Prime + 1.75% (floor 8.0%).
- **Collateral:** First lien on the financed equipment (titled), plus blanket UCC-1 on business assets.
